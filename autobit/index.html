The app

A guided inspection, from first checks to final decision.

AutoBit brings together the details often checked separately: vehicle condition, documents, technical information, photos, category scores and side-by-side comparisons.

What you can do with AutoBit

A simple process for collecting information and turning it into an easy-to-read assessment.

  • More than 80 guided checks to help you cover important details.
  • Overall 0–100 score with results for individual categories.
  • Side-by-side comparison of multiple saved vehicles.
  • Professional PDF reports and an evaluation archive.
  • Local use, without an account or uploading your information.
First the facts.
Then the decision.

AutoBit does not replace a professional inspection. It helps you gather and organize the information worth reviewing before you make a decision.
