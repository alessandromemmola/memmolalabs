DotTap Game

One simple rule.
A faster challenge every time.

DotTap strips gameplay down to its essentials: a dot appears, and you react. Your result depends on how quickly and accurately you can tap.

Instant gameplay

Quick games you can start in seconds, designed to test your focus, coordination and reaction speed.

  • Simple controls: just tap.
  • Personal bests to beat, game after game.
  • Short sessions, even when you only have a few minutes.
  • Up to four profiles to compete on the same device.

Free to play. No subscription.

Classic and Precision are available right away. Rush and Survival can be unlocked as Premium modes with a one-time purchase.

  • Available in English and Italian.
  • No registration required.
  • No user data collected, as stated in the store listing.
  • Premium game modes without recurring subscriptions.
