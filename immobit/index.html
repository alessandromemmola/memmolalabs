The app

A structured evaluation before you decide.

ImmoBit brings property checks, condition, costs, comfort, photos, scores and comparisons together in one guided workflow.

What you can do with ImmoBit

A guided approach that turns a property viewing into an assessment you can understand and compare.

  • More than 60 guided checks to assess a property.
  • Overall score from 0 to 100, plus category-level results.
  • Side-by-side comparisons of multiple saved properties.
  • Professional PDF reports, photos, notes and evaluation history.
  • Local use, with no account or data uploads.
Observe first.
Then compare.

ImmoBit does not replace a qualified inspector or professional. It helps you collect and organize the details worth checking before making a decision.
